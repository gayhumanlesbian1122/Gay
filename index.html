Most servers are still running on a free bot someone set up two years ago — and nobody left who understands it.

Public bots get abandoned, rate-limited, or shut down without warning, and free templates rarely fit how a specific community actually works. Exity exists for the point where a server has outgrown that — where it needs something built for it, by someone who'll still answer when it breaks.

Built for your server, not a template

Every bot and every channel structure starts from how your community actually behaves, not a generic setup with your name swapped in.

Someone answers when it breaks

Discord changes its API, bots go down, roles get misconfigured at 2am. We stay on as maintainers, not just the people who shipped it once.

You own everything you get

Source code, bot tokens, and data stay yours. If you ever want to move to a different team, there's nothing locking you to us.

What we build

Pick one piece or hand us the whole thing — most clients start with a server rebuild or a single bot, then come back for the rest.

01

Server design & setup

Channel structure, roles and permissions, onboarding flow, and moderation settings built around how your community actually behaves.

02

Custom bot development

Moderation, verification, leveling, tickets, economy systems — written for your server specifically, so it does exactly what you asked and nothing you didn't.

03

Integrations & automation

Connecting Discord to the rest of your stack — a website, a game server, a payment provider, a database — so information moves without anyone copy-pasting it.

04

Hosting & maintenance

Bots stay online, get patched when Discord's API changes, and get new features on request — so the server doesn't quietly break six months after launch.
